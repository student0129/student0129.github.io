Greg Palast

Greg Palast is an investigative journalist and the author of the New York Times bestsellers Armed Madhouse and The Best Democracy Money Can Buy. Before turning to journalism he lectured on economics, statistics and investigative reporting at Cambridge, Harvard, the London School of Economics, Columbia and the University of São Paulo.
