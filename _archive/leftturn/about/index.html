About

A Quarterly Journal of Critical Voices

Left Turn is sponsored by the Beyond Capitalism group of the Alliance for Sustainable Communities — Lehigh Valley. It publishes original essays, first-person testimony, primary documents, research dossiers and poetry, alongside work republished from the wider left ecosystem under open licences.

The journal is produced by volunteers. Contributed work does not necessarily represent the views of the Alliance. We welcome comments from readers at leftturn@sustainlv.org.

Licensing and reuse

The journal is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence. You may republish anything here, non-commercially, with attribution and under the same licence. Individual works are also subject to copyright by the author; where a piece carries different terms, they are stated on the article page.

Much of what appears in Left Turn is republished from other outlets that share this licensing culture. Every such piece names its source, its original publication date and a link back, both on the page and in the machine-readable metadata.

Accessibility

This edition is built to be read by screen readers, at any text size, on any device, and in either a light or dark theme. Endnotes appear as margin notes on wide screens and as ordinary endnotes elsewhere. If something here is hard for you to read or use, please tell us — that is a bug, and we will fix it.
