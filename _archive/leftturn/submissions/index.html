For writers

Submissions

We accept articles of 500–2,500 words in electronic format. Please include a short author bio.

What we publish

Original essays and commentary; first-person testimony and witness; primary documents and collective statements; research dossiers with sources; and poetry. We also republish work from other outlets under open licences — if you would like us to consider a piece already published elsewhere, send the link and confirm the licence.

House style

  • 500–2,500 words. Longer pieces are possible for dossiers; write to us first.
  • Send plain text, Markdown, or a word-processor file — not a PDF.
  • Link your sources inline. If you use endnotes, number them; they will be rendered as margin notes on the web and as endnotes in the print edition.
  • A two- or three-sentence author bio, written in the third person.
  • Suggest two or three subject tags if you have a view on them.

What happens next

The editors read everything. Expect a reply within three weeks. Accepted pieces are assigned to a forthcoming issue, but may be published on the site ahead of the issue where the subject is time-sensitive — the issue page will pick them up when it goes out.
