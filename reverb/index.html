Everything a great meeting needs, kept up with for you.

Cards appear as things are said — a one-line answer you can read at a glance, with more behind it when you want it. Mute any kind you don’t need, and a thumbs-down teaches it what this room finds useful.

Ask this

The question worth asking, right now

Every half-minute of talk, ReVerb looks for what would move the meeting forward — the follow-up on what was just said, the number nobody pinned down, the thread nobody pulled — written for your side of the table and addressed to the right person. Mark it Asked, or Skip it and nothing like it comes back.

Answers

The question just asked, answered

When someone asks something answerable, the answer is on screen before the room has to go and look it up — from your background notes, or from the web with sources.

Jargon

Terms explained, quietly

The acronym or concept someone might be nodding along to gets a plain-language note — only when it matters to this discussion — and lands in a glossary for the whole meeting.

Fact check

Claims checked as they’re made

A specific, public claim — what a product can do, a price, a date — is checked against sources, with a clear verdict: correct, partly, or wrong.

To-dos

Who said they’d do what

Commitments become a to-do list with owners and deadlines — updated when someone accepts, hands over or re-dates one, and yours to tick off, fix or add to.

The bigger picture

What the room is missing

A question nobody answered, an argument on its third lap, two people meaning different things by one word, a decision that contradicts an earlier one, time slipping away from the agenda — or someone who hasn’t been heard from in a while.

Summary

The meeting at a glance

Topics, decisions, open threads, who holds which view, the numbers said, who’s been doing the talking, and a checklist of what this kind of meeting needs to cover — kept current all meeting.

Chat

Ask the meeting anything

“What did we decide about pricing?” Answers come only from what was said, with the minute it was said — tap it to see the moment. It can look across your saved past meetings too.

Corrections

Fix what it misheard

Correct a line, tell it who a speaker really was, or note what it got wrong — or, if you recorded, have the recording transcribed again with every voice told apart. The summary and the chat follow.

Your context

Knows what kind of meeting it’s in

A decision, a 1:1, a sales call, a job interview, a user interview, a class — pick one and ReVerb knows what it’s for, what it needs to cover and what a good question sounds like. Add what you want out of it, who’s there or the calendar invite, and every card gets sharper.

Video calls

Hears the whole call

On Zoom, Meet or Teams in the browser, share the meeting’s tab and ReVerb hears everyone — not just your microphone — even with headphones on.

Recap

Send the outcome in one click

Decisions, to-dos and open questions, ready to copy, email, or share as a link that opens a clean recap — the recap travels in the link itself, stored nowhere.

Follow-ups

Every meeting builds on the last

Keep a meeting’s recap, and the next one starts with what was decided, what’s still open and who owes what already in the brief.

Let it listen

Put your laptop or phone where it can hear the room. Cards arrive as the conversation moves.

  • Free transcription in Chrome and Edge
  • High-accuracy mode for names and jargon
  • On a video call, the meeting’s tab as well
  • Optional recording, saved to your disk

Leave with the outcome

Pause and pick up again any time. When it’s over, everything is there.

  • A recap to copy, email or share by link
  • The full transcript, with who said what
  • A chat to ask the meeting follow-ups

Your meeting stays yours.

Kept in your browser

The transcript, summary, to-dos and chat are saved on your device, so a closed tab or a crash loses nothing — and past meetings stay only if you keep them. ReVerb doesn’t keep your meetings on its servers.

Gone when you say so

End session erases the meeting from the screen and from your browser. Audio is only recorded if you ask, to a file you choose — and only leaves your device if you ask ReVerb to improve the transcript from it.

On a shared computer?

Cleared. Nothing from ReVerb is saved in this browser now.

Tell the room

What’s said is sent to AI services to be transcribed and analysed — the privacy policy has the detail. Let people know you’re using ReVerb before you start.
