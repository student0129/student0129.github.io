New Session

Pick the kind of meeting and start. Everything else is optional and only makes ReVerb sharper.

Meeting

ReVerb suggests questions to ask, tracks what this kind of meeting needs to cover, and flags what matters as it’s said. Names you list help it spell them and tell who said what.

Listening

Press Record, then read this aloud in your normal voice: This is my voice for ReVerb. I’m recording a short sample so it can tell when I’m the one talking in a meeting.

Your sample stays in this browser. During a meeting it goes, with short stretches of the meeting’s audio, through our server to OpenAI to match voices; nothing is kept on our server.

More Options

Terms are spelling hints for the speech model. With an agenda or a length, ReVerb tells you when time is going to the wrong thing or running out. Questions the material answers get answered from it, and anything said that reverses a decision recorded in it is flagged.
