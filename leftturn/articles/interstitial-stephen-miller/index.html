We set the terms and conditions

Stephen Miller

Fall / Winter 2025-2026 Vol. 6, No. 1–2 Page 9 1 min read
Text size

We’re a superpower, and under President Trump, we are going to conduct ourselves as a superpower. It is absurd that we would allow a nation in our own backyard to become the supplier of resources to our adversaries but not to us… We are in charge because we have the United States military stationed outside the country. We set the terms and conditions. We have a complete embargo on all of their oil and their ability to do commerce.

— Stephen Miller, deputy White House chief of staff and Homeland Security adviser
