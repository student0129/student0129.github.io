But Do You Condemn Them?

Winter 2024 Vol. , No.  2 min read
Text size

Idris Settles

We see your people murdered
Time and time again
And we watch your children suffer
But do you condemn them?

We are witnessing injustice
Against your women and your men
We see it with our own eyes
But do you condemn them?

For decades you’ve been wronged
This didn’t just begin
We hear about your struggle
But do you condemn them?

You’re submerged in darkness
A deep place where you can’t swim
And we hear your cry for help
But do you condemn them?

We see light shine from your people
But we downplay it as dim
We’re really jealous of your courage
But do you condemn them?

The hypocrisy of our ways
Sticks out just like a stem
And we never fail to ask you
“But do you condemn them?”

We praise our president
Your oppressors get support from him
We know this to be unfair
But do you condemn them?

Your hope for all humanity
Is starting to look so slim
We have the power to help you
But do you condemn them?

Your land which is so sacred
And precious like a gem
Is being taken from you
But do you condemn them?

We aren’t helping you now
And we didn’t help back then
We know it’s a stupid question
But do you condemn them?

You don’t ask for much
Just for the occupation to end
That land has belonged to you
But do you condemn them?

We claim we can’t help you
But take your enemies as our friend
We reject calls for ceasefire
But do you condemn them?

We justify the killing
Claiming that’s not what they “intend”
We can’t escape the truth
But do you condemn them?

We send billions to your attackers
So we must have lots to spend
We deflect responsibility
But do you condemn them?

We say we can’t do much
All we do is pretend
We’re on the wrong side of history
But do you condemn them?

We never have an answer
Against these claims we can’t defend
So as a copout we wonder
“But do you condemn them?”

It’s not like us to do what’s right
Something you can’t comprehend
We couldn’t tell you ourselves
But do you condemn them?

We’re supporting a genocide
A fact that we fail to contend
And while this is true, we want to know
“But do you condemn them?”

Idris is a sophomore at Lehigh Carbon Community College.
