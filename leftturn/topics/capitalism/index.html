Venezuela: It's Much More Than Oil

by Don Fitz

Wars for oil and wars for alternative energy are the same war. On the Caracazo, the racial structure that made Venezuela vulnerable, and why a genuine solution requires degrowth in the rich world rather than only the arrest of a president.

The Environmental Crisis Is a Capitalist Crisis

by Tricontinental: Institute for Social Research

A dossier on the class character of the environmental crisis: why green capitalism cannot solve a crisis capitalism created, and a minimum agenda — seven headings, drawn up with Brazil's Landless Workers' Movement — for confronting it.

by Gary Olson

From the 1953 formulation of "Common Prosperity" through Deng's market turn to Xi's New Era: an account of how the CPC has adapted Marxism to concrete conditions, and what "advanced socialism" by 2049 would require.
