Interstitial

We set the terms and conditions

by Stephen Miller

Stephen Miller, deputy White House chief of staff and Homeland Security adviser, on Venezuela.

 Essay

Abduction in Caracas

by Tariq Ali

Two decades before US forces seized Nicolás Maduro, Hugo Chávez predicted the method: the Noriega formula, a narco-trafficking indictment, and then anything goes. What the abduction reveals about Washington, about the Bolivarian regime's own hollowing out, and about what may come next for Cuba.

 Poetry

Ms Maxwell and The Art of The Deal

by Raymond Nat Turner

A satirical catalogue of demands, in which a prisoner's terms for silence expand until they take in the whole apparatus — the agencies, the bases, the tariffs, the burial with honours.

 Commentary

"The Days of the United States Propping Up the Entire World Order Like Atlas Are Over."

by Gary Olson

A quick reading of the new National Security Strategy: ghoulish, repetitious, sometimes incoherent — and refreshingly honest. The ideological cover is gone, and what is left is a document that quietly concedes American decline.

 Essay

Sorry, White Nationalists

by Juan R. I. Cole

Whiteness is an objectively meaningless construct with a checkered history — and the genetic record is unkind to those who invoke it. Ancient DNA shows most Europeans were dark-skinned until the first millennium BC.

 Essay

Mass Murder as "Public Service" in "Our Democracy"

by Michael Smith

Elliott Abrams is back, urging Trump to finish the job in Venezuela. A record — El Salvador, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Iran-Contra — and the 1995 Charlie Rose exchange with Allan Nairn, reproduced in full.

 Essay

Mokhiber on UN Security Council Abomination

by Craig Mokhiber

Resolution 2803 hands control of Gaza to a US-chaired "Board of Peace" and a proxy occupation force, implicitly rejects a series of ICJ findings, and grants Washington a veto over Palestinian self-determination. In one minute of voting, the Security Council lost all legitimacy.

 Document

A Calculated Attempt to Erase the Horizon of Palestinian Liberation

by Cabinet of the Progressive International

The resolution installs two bodies with sweeping authority over Gaza's political, economic and security life, neither accountable to Palestinians. A statement rejecting it and calling on progressive forces to name it for what it is.
