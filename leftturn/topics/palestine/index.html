Palestine

The genocide in Gaza, the occupation, and the international machinery around them.

10 pieces

Essay

Mokhiber on UN Security Council Abomination

by Craig Mokhiber

Resolution 2803 hands control of Gaza to a US-chaired "Board of Peace" and a proxy occupation force, implicitly rejects a series of ICJ findings, and grants Washington a veto over Palestinian self-determination. In one minute of voting, the Security Council lost all legitimacy.

 Document

Gaza Tribunal Jury of Conscience

by The Jury of Conscience

The concluding statement of the Gaza Tribunal's Jury of Conscience, naming and defining the constituent crimes — domicide, ecocide, reprocide, scholasticide, politicide — and finding Western governments and non-state actors complicit.

 Testimony

My Testimony at the Gaza Tribunal in Istanbul

by Maura Finkelstein

Testimony delivered as an expert witness on university complicity in genocide, organised around three features: investments, partnerships, and the silencing of dissent. The author is one of the sixty-one faculty recorded as having faced retaliation for advocating for Palestine.

 Essay

Some Thoughts on Trump's Gaza 'Peace Plan'

by Faramarz Farbod

The plan's clauses look comprehensive, and it has already produced a ceasefire. But it avoids the central question — the political rights of Palestinians and a sovereign Palestinian state — and mistakes the silencing of guns for peace.

 Interstitial

We are humans who want nothing more than to live a normal life

Plestia Alaqad, journalist and author of The Eyes of Gaza: A Diary of Resilience.

 Commentary

The UN Has Voted to Put the Occupation of Iraq in Charge of Gaza

by David Swanson

The Security Council has voted for an Orwellian "Peace Board" concocted by Donald Trump and run by Tony Blair. A to-do list for anyone unwilling to accept the normalization of not just genocide but colonialism.

 Essay

The Return Home

On domicide as one of the most powerful tools of genocide — the retraction of yesterday, the threat on today — and on the Sameer Project's campaign to help people return north to homes that are almost certainly gone.

 Interstitial

A cease-fire might stop the missiles, but the trauma remains

A Calculated Attempt to Erase the Horizon of Palestinian Liberation

The resolution installs two bodies with sweeping authority over Gaza's political, economic and security life, neither accountable to Palestinians. A statement rejecting it and calling on progressive forces to name it for what it is.
