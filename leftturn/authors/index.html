Elaheh Farmand

Contributing editor · 4 pieces

Elaheh Farmand immigrated to the U.S. when she was eleven, leaving her birth country, Iran. In 2016 she founded Immigrants & Exile, a performance series inviting people from all disciplines and backgrounds to share their stories of immigration, nostalgia, longing and exile. Her poetry and prose have appeared in Left Turn and Recenter Press.
