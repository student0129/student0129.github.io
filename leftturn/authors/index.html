Contributors

Who writes here

Contributors accumulate a body of work across issues rather than a scatter of bylines. Each page gathers everything a writer has published in the journal.

Tariq Ali

1 piece

Tariq Ali is a writer and editor at New Left Review. He has written fifty books on history and politics and seven novels. You Can't Please All: Memoirs 1980–2024 is his latest.

Juan R. I. Cole

1 piece

Juan R. I. Cole teaches History at the University of Michigan and is the author of several books including Muhammad: Prophet of Peace Amid the Clash of Empires and The New Arabs: How the Millennial Generation is Changing the Middle East.

Faramarz Farbod

Editor · 1 piece

Faramarz Farbod, a native of Iran, teaches politics at Moravian University in Pennsylvania. He is the founder of the Beyond Capitalism Working Group, editor of its publication Left Turn, and coeditor of Dissident Voice.

Elaheh Farmand

Contributing editor · 1 piece

Elaheh Farmand immigrated to the U.S. when she was eleven, leaving her birth country, Iran. In 2016 she founded Immigrants & Exile, a performance series inviting people from all disciplines and backgrounds to share their stories of immigration, nostalgia, longing and exile. Her poetry and prose have appeared in Left Turn and Recenter Press.

Marwa Fatafta

1 piece

Marwa Fatafta is a Palestinian writer, researcher and policy analyst based in Berlin. She leads Access Now's work on digital rights in the Middle East and North Africa.

Maura Finkelstein

2 pieces

Maura Finkelstein is a writer, ethnographer, editor and a former Associate Professor of Anthropology. She is the author of The Archive of Loss: Lively Ruination in Mill Land Mumbai, published by Duke University Press in 2019.

Don Fitz

1 piece

Don Fitz has taught Environmental Psychology at Washington University and is the author of Cuban Health Care: The Ongoing Revolution. He is Outreach Coordinator for the Green Party of St. Louis and sits on the editorial board of Green Social Thought.

Ashley Gate

1 piece

Ashley Gate is a researcher in the Quincy Institute's Democratizing Foreign Policy program.

William D. Hartung

1 piece

William D. Hartung is a senior research fellow at the Quincy Institute for Responsible Statecraft and the author, with Ben Freeman, of The Trillion Dollar War Machine: How Runaway Military Spending Drives America into Foreign Wars and Bankrupts Us at Home.

Craig Mokhiber

1 piece

Craig Mokhiber is an international human rights lawyer and former senior United Nations official. He left the UN in October 2023, penning a widely read letter that warned of genocide in Gaza, criticised the international response and called for a new approach to Palestine and Israel based on equality, human rights and international law.

Gary Olson

Contributing editor · 2 pieces

Gary Olson is professor emeritus of Political Science at Moravian University. He is the author of Empathy Imperiled: Capitalism, Culture, and the Brain, How the World Works, U.S. Foreign Policy and the Third World Peasant and The Other Europe.

Greg Palast

1 piece

Greg Palast is an investigative journalist and the author of the New York Times bestsellers Armed Madhouse and The Best Democracy Money Can Buy. Before turning to journalism he lectured on economics, statistics and investigative reporting at Cambridge, Harvard, the London School of Economics, Columbia and the University of São Paulo.

Kim Petersen

1 piece

Kim Petersen is an independent writer and co-editor of Dissident Voice.

Vijay Prashad

1 piece

Vijay Prashad is an Indian historian, editor and journalist. He is a writing fellow and chief correspondent at Globetrotter, an editor of LeftWord Books and the director of Tricontinental: Institute for Social Research. He has written more than twenty books, including The Darker Nations and The Poorer Nations.

Michael Smith

1 piece

Michael Smith is the author of Portraits of Empire. He co-blogs with Frank Scott at legalienate.blogspot.com.

David Swanson

1 piece

David Swanson is an author, activist, journalist and radio host. He is director of WorldBeyondWar.org and campaign coordinator for RootsAction.org. His books include War Is A Lie. He hosts Talk Nation Radio.

Raymond Nat Turner

1 piece

Former forklift driver, warehouse worker and janitor, Raymond Nat Turner is a New York City poet, Black Agenda Report's Poet-in-Residence, and founder and co-leader of the jazz-poetry ensemble UpSurge!NYC.
