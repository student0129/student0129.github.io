1. Who we are

Clarity (the "App") is provided by Promontory AI, LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), a limited liability company organised in the United States. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, retain, and protect information when you use the App. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Promontory AI, LLC is the controller of the personal information described in this Policy. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

Account information you provide

When you create an account, you provide your name, email address, and phone number, and you choose a password. We store your password only as a salted cryptographic hash — we never store or have access to the password itself.

Profile and demographic information you provide

During onboarding and in Settings, you may optionally provide details that let the App place your position in context — for example your date or year of birth, gender, ZIP or postal code, city, state, and country, education level, occupation and industry, years of work experience, household size, marital status, and number of dependents. These fields are optional; the App works without them, though comparison features are less precise if they are left blank.

Financial information you provide

The App is built around figures you enter yourself: income, expenses, assets, liabilities and debts, savings, and the goals you set. You decide what to enter, at what level of detail, and you can change or remove any of it at any time.

Content you create in the App

If you use the built-in assistant, we store the messages you send and the replies you receive so that a conversation can continue across sessions. See AI features and Data retention.

Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the message, your email address, and anything else you choose to include, and we keep it as needed to respond and to keep a record of the request.

Information collected automatically

When the App communicates with our servers, we automatically receive limited technical information: your device type and operating system version, the App version, your IP address (used to route the connection, apply rate limits, and detect abuse), the language and region the App is set to, and server-side diagnostic logs of requests and errors. We use this to operate the service, keep it secure, and fix problems.

We also keep security audit records of significant account events — such as sign-ins, password changes, and password reset attempts. Each record holds the event type, the time, the account it relates to, and the IP address the request came from. We use these records only to secure accounts, investigate suspicious activity, and meet our legal obligations.

3. What we don't collect

To be explicit, the App does not:

  • Connect to your bank, brokerage, or card accounts, or ask for banking credentials. Every figure in the App is one you entered yourself.
  • Collect your precise or GPS location. Where you provide a ZIP or postal code, it is a value you typed, used only for regional context.
  • Include third-party advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs.
  • Use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, cookies for advertising, or any form of behavioural profiling for marketing.
  • Access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, calendar, or health data.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features;
  • Calculate the scores, metrics, projections, and insights the App is designed to deliver;
  • Compare your position against public statistical benchmarks for households with similar characteristics;
  • Generate written explanations and assistant replies about your own figures;
  • Present the App in your chosen language;
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • Respond to your requests and provide customer support;
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical problems, fraud, abuse, or misuse; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not use your personal financial information to train third-party AI models. See AI features.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR / UK GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract — to create and maintain your account and deliver the features you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, diagnose faults, and improve the App, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — for optional information you choose to provide, such as demographic details. You may withdraw consent at any time by removing that information or deleting your account.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

6. AI features

Some features — written explanations of your scores, summaries, translations, and the in-app assistant — are produced with the help of a third-party large language model provider (currently Anthropic, PBC). How this works matters, so we are specific:

  • Financial figures only. The information sent for processing consists of financial metrics and the text of your own questions. Your name, email address, and phone number are not sent.
  • No training on your data. Under our agreement with the provider, information sent through the API is not used to train their models.
  • Conversation memory. So that the assistant can follow a conversation, recent messages from your chat history are replayed as context along with a current snapshot of your figures.
  • Generated output is stored. Explanations and summaries are cached against your account, per language, so the App does not regenerate them on every screen.
  • It is not advice. AI-generated output is informational only, may be incomplete or wrong, and is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. See our Terms of Service.

You can delete your assistant conversation from within the App at any time, and it is purged automatically on the schedule set out in Data retention.

8. No sale or sharing for advertising

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended, or under comparable laws in other U.S. states. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

9. Public reference data

To provide economic context and benchmarks, we ingest publicly available statistical data from sources such as the Federal Reserve Economic Data service, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Bureau of Economic Analysis, and the U.S. Census Bureau. This is aggregate public data about the economy and about households in general. Your personal information is never sent to these sources, and comparisons are drawn against published statistics — never against other Clarity users' individual data.

10. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption of traffic between the App and our servers, encrypted connections to our database, salted password hashing, authenticated and rate-limited API access, and restricted internal access to production systems.

On your device

The App keeps a local copy of your information so it works smoothly and can hold changes made while you are offline until they sync. Your authentication tokens and account record are stored in the iOS Keychain, and the cached financial profile — income, balances, net worth — is encrypted at rest with AES-GCM under a 256-bit key that is generated on your device, held in the Keychain, and never leaves it or is transmitted to us. Removing the App removes this local copy.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protecting your account also depends on you: use a strong, unique password, and tell us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

12. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Access and review the information in your account from within the App;
  • Correct or update your profile and financial information at any time;
  • Delete individual entries or your assistant conversation, or your entire account — see Deleting your account;
  • Withdraw optional information such as demographic fields by clearing them; and
  • Contact us at privacy@promontoryai.com with any privacy request.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the time required by applicable law. To protect your account, we may need to verify your identity — usually by confirming control of the email address on the account. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the App: open Settings, scroll to Delete Account, and confirm.

This permanently removes your account record and everything linked to it — profile and demographic details, financial entries, goals, calculated metrics, cached insights and summaries, saved scenarios, and your assistant history — from our database. It happens immediately, it is not a deactivation, and it cannot be undone.

Two narrow exceptions, both required to run the service responsibly. Residual copies may persist for a limited period in encrypted backups, from which they are overwritten on our normal backup cycle. And our security audit records — sign-ins, password changes, the deletion itself — are kept for fraud prevention and legal compliance, but the link to you is severed at the moment of deletion: the account reference is cleared, leaving a dated event and nothing that identifies you.

If you cannot get into the App, email privacy@promontoryai.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. Deleting the App from your device removes the local copy described in Data security, but does not by itself delete your account from our servers.
