Common questions

How do I delete my account and my data?

Open the App, go to Settings, scroll to Delete Account, and confirm. Your account and everything linked to it — profile and demographic details, financial entries, goals, metrics, cached insights, and assistant history — is erased from our database immediately. It's a real deletion, not a deactivation, and it can't be undone.

If you can't get into the App, email privacy@promontoryai.com from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.

Does Clarity connect to my bank?

No. Clarity never asks for banking credentials and does not link to bank, brokerage, or card accounts. Every figure in the App is one you entered yourself, which is also why you can use it without handing over account access.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email address and tap Forgot password? We'll email you a link to choose a new one. The link works once and expires after an hour; if it lapses, just request another.

Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes. If you no longer have access to the email address on your account, contact clarity@promontoryai.com.

Is my financial information sold or used for advertising?

No. We don't sell personal information and we don't share it for advertising. The App ships without third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What does the assistant see when I ask it a question?

It sees your financial metrics and the text of your own messages. Your name, email address, and phone number are not sent for processing. Recent messages are replayed so the conversation can follow on, and the history is purged automatically after 30 days — or sooner, if you clear it yourself.

See AI features in the Privacy Policy.

How are my Risk and Opportunity scores calculated?

Both are derived from the figures you enter, combined with published economic data. Risk weighs your exposure — runway, debt load, concentration, and current economic conditions. Opportunity weighs your room to act — surplus, flexibility, and the levers available to you. Open either score in the App to see the components and the arithmetic behind the number.

Scores are informational only and are not financial advice. See the Terms of Service.

Who do you compare me against?

Published statistics from U.S. federal sources — the Federal Reserve Economic Data service, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Bureau of Economic Analysis, and the Census Bureau. Comparisons are drawn against aggregate public data for households with similar characteristics, never against other Clarity users' individual information.

Which languages does Clarity support?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, and Persian — including right-to-left layout. Change it in Settings and the whole App switches immediately, including generated explanations and insights.

Which devices does Clarity run on?

Clarity is an iPhone app and requires iOS 18.0 or later. It's available from the App Store.

I found a bug, or something looks wrong.

Please tell us at clarity@promontoryai.com. It helps a lot if you include your device model, your iOS version, the App version from Settings, and what you were doing when it happened.
