01 — The scores

Two numbers,
honestly derived.

Risk measures how exposed you are — runway, debt load, concentration, and the shape of the wider economy. It moves when your situation moves, not when a market headline does.

Opportunity measures the room you have to act — surplus, flexibility, and the levers actually available to you right now.

Every score comes with the arithmetic behind it. Nothing is a black box you have to take on faith.

Statements

A real balance sheet

Assets and liabilities on one side, income and expenses on the other. Enter a single estimate to get moving, or itemise every line when you want the full picture.

Goals

Targets that track themselves

Set what you're saving toward and Clarity keeps the progress current as your numbers change — no spreadsheet to maintain.

Scenarios

Stress-test the plan

Lose the income. Watch rates climb. See a downturn arrive. Clarity re-runs your position against the shock and shows what actually breaks.

Debt

Know what to pay first

Every balance ranked by what it truly costs you, with the payoff order that clears the most interest.

Benchmarks

Context, not comparison

See how your position reads against public federal data for households like yours — drawn from national statistical sources, never from other users.

Assistant

Ask in plain language

A built-in assistant that can see your numbers and answer questions about them — grounded in your actual position, not generic advice.

02 — Privacy

Your finances aren't the product.

  • No advertising or analytics SDKs. The app ships without third-party trackers.
  • We don't sell your personal information. Not to anyone, for any purpose.
  • The assistant sees figures, not identity. Financial metrics are sent for processing — your name and email are not.
  • Deletion is one tap. Settings → Delete Account erases your account and its data immediately — not a deactivation.
